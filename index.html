About

I am Director of Engineering at Visa Inc., where I lead the Risk and Identity Solutions engineering organization building fraud prevention and transaction authorization platforms. Over nearly 19 years in software engineering, my work has centered on real-time risk systems operating at global payments scale. I am co-inventor on 11+ U.S. patents in fraud detection — several cited as prior art in later patents by Capital One and Mastercard — and I serve as a peer reviewer for machine learning venues including the Transactions on Machine Learning Research, IEEE conferences, and NeurIPS workshops.
